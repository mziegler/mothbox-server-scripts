At Manu Biostation, we're using AI-powered Mothbox insect camera traps to monitor insect diversity in Amazon rainforest restoration sites. Each Mothbox runs on solar power and connects to the internet via fiber optic cable, letting it operate independently for the long term.

This page shows the latest photos from each Mothbox device in real time!

Move your cursor over the image to zoom in

Daily schedule

The Mothboxes are online every other hour, from 6pm to 5am Peru Standard Time. (The cameras and attractor lights turn off intermittently so the same insects don't linger on the sheet all night, letting us sample a wider variety as the night goes on.)

The Mothboxes will tend to have more insects towards the end of the hour, after the lights have been on for a while. Weather and moon phase also play a big role in how many insects show up. Watch for the mix of visitors to shift throughout the night as temperatures cool.
