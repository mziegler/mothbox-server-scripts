Manu Biostation Mothbox Livestream

At Manu Biostation, we are using AI-powered Mothbox insect camera traps to monitor insect diversity in Amazon rainforest restoration sites. To run long-term, each of the Mothboxes are powered by solar panels and connected to the Internet by fiber optic cables.

This page shows the latest photos from each Mothbox device in real time!

Move your cursor over the image to zoom in

Daily schedule

The Mothboxes are online every-other-hour, from 6pm to 5am Peru Standard Time. (The cameras and attractor lights turn off intermittently, so the same insects don't stay on the sheet all night, so we can sample more different insects throughout the night.)
